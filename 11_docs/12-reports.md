# Purpose

The Reports Module provides complete operational, financial, analytical and executive reports for Dhara Photography ERP Pro V2.

Every report shall use live ERP data and support analysis, filtering, exporting and business decision making.

---

# Objectives

The Reports Module shall

- Generate operational reports
- Generate financial reports
- Generate customer reports
- Generate employee reports
- Generate equipment reports
- Generate executive reports
- Support AI business insights
- Support scheduled reports
- Support White Label ERP
- Support Multi Branch Operations

---

# Core Principles

## Read Only Reporting

Reports shall never modify business data.

Reports only display

- Live Information
- Historical Information
- Business Analytics
- KPIs
- Charts
- Trends

Business Rules

- Reports shall always use live database records.
- Editing business data from reports is prohibited.
- Reports follow Role Based Access Control (RBAC).

---

## Live Data

Every report shall use live ERP information.

Supported Modules

- Booking
- Clients
- CRM
- Staff
- Equipment
- Inventory
- Accounts
- Dashboard

Business Rules

- Reports always use latest approved records.
- Archived records included only when selected.
- Cached reports refreshed automatically.
