1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

The Deployment Architecture defines how the Dhara Photography ERP Platform
is installed, configured, deployed, maintained and scaled across different
environments.

The architecture supports both small photography studios and future
enterprise deployments while ensuring reliability, scalability and
business continuity.
