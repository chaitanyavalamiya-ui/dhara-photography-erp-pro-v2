# ADR-0001: Configurable RBAC hierarchy for Version 1

- Status: Approved
- Date: 2026-08-07
- Decision owner: Product Owner

## Context

Version 1 needs a simpler operating hierarchy than the historic role catalogue,
without coupling authorization to role names. All permissions, role assignments,
and scopes must be editable configuration held in the database.

## Decision

The initial configurable hierarchy is Owner, Admin, Manager, Staff, and Viewer.
The hierarchy is an initial configuration, not an authorization rule in code.

- Owner has full access.
- Admin has nearly all operational permissions, but cannot transfer ownership or
  delete the company.
- Manager manages day-to-day operations.
- Staff has limited operational permissions.
- Viewer has read-only access.

The system authorizes requests through database-driven permissions and scope
assignments. Role names must never be used as hardcoded authorization checks.
The UI loads permissions dynamically, while backend authorization remains the
authoritative enforcement point.

## Consequences

- Role, permission, role-permission, and user-role-assignment records are part
  of the Version 1 schema.
- Company and ownership-protection operations require explicit permissions and
  protected business rules in the future backend implementation.
- Roles and permissions can be extended or renamed without a source-code change.
