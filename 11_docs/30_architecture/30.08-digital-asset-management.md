1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

The Digital Asset Management (DAM) system is responsible for managing every
digital file inside the Dhara Photography ERP Platform.

The DAM module stores, organizes, tracks, secures and retrieves photographs,
videos, documents and other digital assets throughout their lifecycle.

The architecture is designed to support enterprise-scale storage while
remaining fast, secure and searchable.
