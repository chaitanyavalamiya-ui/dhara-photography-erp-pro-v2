============================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Dhara Photography ERP Platform is an Enterprise Grade Business Management
Platform designed exclusively for Photography, Videography and Event
Production Companies.

This platform is not intended to become a simple billing software or a
booking application.

The objective is to build a complete business operating system capable of
managing every department of a professional photography company from Lead
Generation to Final Delivery.

The platform will eventually evolve into a SaaS product capable of serving
thousands of photography studios across India and international markets.

Every architecture decision taken in this project must support scalability,
maintainability, configurability, security and long-term business growth.

This document represents the highest level vision of the product.

Every future document, database table, API endpoint, UI screen and workflow
must follow this vision.

============================================================
2. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

Our vision is to build the world's most complete Photography ERP Platform.

The software should eliminate manual work, spreadsheets, disconnected tools
and repetitive processes.

The platform should help studio owners spend less time managing operations
and more time growing their business and serving clients.

The system should become a centralized operating platform where every
business activity is managed from one place.

The ERP should remain useful for a small studio with one employee as well as
large organizations operating multiple branches and hundreds of staff
members.

============================================================
3. MISSION
============================================================

Our mission is to provide a single intelligent platform capable of managing
every business operation performed inside a professional photography studio.

The software must automate repetitive work, standardize workflows,

5. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

The entire platform follows these core principles.

1. Build Once

The architecture should be designed correctly from the beginning to minimize
future rewrites.

2. Scale Forever

The system should support growth from a single studio to thousands of
organizations.

3. Configuration Over Hardcoding

Business rules, pricing, services, permissions and workflows should be
configurable through settings whenever practical.

4. Database First

Business rules should be reflected in the database architecture before user
interfaces are developed.

5. API First

Every business function should be exposed through well-designed APIs so that
web, mobile and future clients can use the same backend.

6. Security By Design

Security must be considered during architecture rather than added later.

7. Workflow Driven

The platform should guide users through standardized workflows instead of
allowing inconsistent manual processes.

8. Future Ready

Every major design decision should consider future expansion including AI,
automation, analytics and multi-tenant SaaS deployment.
