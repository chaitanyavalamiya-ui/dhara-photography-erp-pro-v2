# Purpose

The Clients Module manages the complete customer lifecycle of Dhara Photography ERP Pro V2.

Every client interaction, booking, payment, communication and relationship shall remain permanently available.

One client may continue business with the studio for many years through multiple events.

---

# Objectives

The Clients Module shall

- Maintain complete customer profile
- Maintain lifetime booking history
- Maintain financial history
- Improve customer relationship
- Support CRM automation
- Support AI Customer Insights
- Support Mobile Application
- Support Client Portal
- Support White Label ERP

---

# Core Principles

## Lifetime Customer

A client is never treated as a single booking.

The ERP maintains the customer's complete lifetime relationship.

---

## Archive Instead of Delete

Clients must never be permanently deleted.

Archived clients remain available for

- Reports
- Financial History
- Booking History
- CRM Timeline

---

## Unique Identity

Every client receives

- Client ID
- CRM ID (Future)
- QR Code (Future)

Client IDs must never be reused.
