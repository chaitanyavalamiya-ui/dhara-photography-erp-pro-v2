1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

This document defines how a professional photography business operates.

The purpose of this document is to convert real-world business operations
into standardized software workflows.

Every module, database table, API endpoint and user interface inside the
ERP platform must follow the business architecture defined here.

The software should adapt to the business.

The business should never adapt to software limitations.

============================================================
11. BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

All quotations must be approved before booking confirmation.

Every confirmed booking must receive a unique booking number.

Advance payment should be recorded before event planning begins.

Equipment should be assigned before the shoot date.

Staff assignments must be completed before the event.

Editing should begin only after RAW data backup verification.

Album printing should start only after client approval.

Final delivery should occur only after quality verification.

Project closure requires balance payment confirmation.

Archived projects must remain searchable.

23. BUSINESS POLICIES
============================================================

The platform must enforce standardized business policies across all
organizations.

Core Business Policies

• Every client must have a unique customer record.

• Every booking must have a unique booking number.

• Every financial transaction must be traceable.

• Every project must follow the approved workflow.

• Every payment must generate a receipt.

• Every invoice must remain immutable after final approval.

• Every equipment assignment must be recorded.

• Every delivery must be acknowledged.

• Every archived project must remain searchable.

• Every important action must be logged.
