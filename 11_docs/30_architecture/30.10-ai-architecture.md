============================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

The AI Architecture defines how Artificial Intelligence is integrated into
the Dhara Photography ERP Platform.

The objective is to improve productivity, reduce manual work, assist
decision making and automate repetitive business processes while keeping
users in control.

AI should assist users rather than replace business decisions.

============================================================
2. AI OBJECTIVES
============================================================

The AI architecture has the following objectives.

• Reduce Manual Work

• Improve Business Decisions

• Increase Productivity

• Improve Customer Experience

• Automate Repetitive Tasks

• Improve Search

• Improve Analytics

• Improve Planning

• Future AI Expansion
