============================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
============================================================

The Workflow Engine defines how every business process moves from one stage
to another inside the Dhara Photography ERP Platform.

Every department, module and employee must follow standardized workflows.

No project should bypass mandatory workflow stages without authorization.

============================================================
2. WORKFLOW OBJECTIVES
============================================================

The workflow engine has the following objectives.

• Standardize Operations

• Reduce Manual Errors

• Improve Team Coordination

• Increase Automation

• Improve Tracking

• Faster Delivery

• Better Accountability

• Configurable Workflows

• AI Ready Automation
