# Purpose

Manage all employees, freelancers and outsourced team members involved in photography projects.

Track profile, assignments, attendance, salary, expenses, performance and history.

# Business Rules

- Every staff member has a unique Employee ID.
- Staff roles come from Settings.
- Assignment conflict not allowed.
- Staff linked with completed projects cannot be deleted.
- Archive instead of Delete.
- Every assignment is recorded in Activity Log.

---

# Validation Rules

- Name is mandatory.
- Mobile is mandatory.
- Role is mandatory.
- Joining Date is mandatory.
- Duplicate mobile warning.
