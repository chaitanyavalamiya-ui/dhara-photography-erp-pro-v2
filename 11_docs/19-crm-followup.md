# Purpose

The CRM & Follow-up Management Module manages the complete customer relationship lifecycle from first enquiry to repeat business.

The module provides intelligent lead management, communication tracking, quotation management, follow-up automation, customer retention and long-term relationship management.

The CRM Module shall

- Manage enquiries
- Improve lead conversion

- Maintain communication history

- Track customer lifecycle

- Support White Label ERP
